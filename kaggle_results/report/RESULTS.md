## 6. Caveats

- Held-out data: 1 of 200 eval prompts also appears in training (ids [150]), and 0 reference answers do. Too few to matter, but Dolly has repeated templates, so a correct answer can come from a learned pattern.
- Summarization: 5 of 11 full_masked summaries copy 60+ characters from the prompt, so its high judge score partly rewards copying, not summarizing.
- Base model: it was never trained to stop, so it fills the token limit. Of its 71 ties with full_masked, nearly all verdicts say both answers were wrong or incoherent.
- Reference answers: some Dolly references are themselves wrong (#121 calls Sandpipers extinct), and the judge treats them as a guide.
- Statistics: judge ranges are normal-approximation 95% intervals over 200 prompts, not corrected for multiple comparisons. Each ablation has 2 seeds and each scaling size 1.
